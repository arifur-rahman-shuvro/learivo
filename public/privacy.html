Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Learivo is a private question bank and exam practice app at https://learivo.pages.dev. This page says what it stores and who can see it.

What Learivo stores

  • Your email address and, if you use an email account, a password (kept by Supabase in hashed form, never readable by Learivo).
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account email and name, so you can sign in. Learivo asks Google for nothing else.
  • What you create in the app: questions, notes, flashcards, glossary terms, collections, saved exams, practice and exam results, your confidence ratings and mistake reasons, and your settings.

Where it is kept

In a database run by Supabase (supabase.com). Each person can read and change only their own rows; this is enforced by the database itself. A copy of recent data is also kept in your own browser so the app works offline, and it is removed when you sign out or use "Clear data on this device".

Who can see it

Only you. Learivo has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of what you study, and it does not sell or share your data. The person who runs the app can technically access the database as an administrator, as with any hosted service, and will not look at your content except to fix a fault you ask about.

Optional services

  • AI helpers. If you add your own AI provider key, it stays in your browser on your device and is never sent to Learivo. Question text you choose to send goes to the provider you picked, under that provider's rules.
  • Crash reports. If switched on, reports describe the kind of error only. They contain no question text, notes or personal details.

Backups and deleting your data

You can download all your data at any time from Menu, Back up and restore. You can delete your account and everything in it from Account, Delete account. Deleting removes your data from the live database. Encrypted database backups kept by the owner are removed after 30 days.

Contact

Questions about your data, or a request to delete it: learivo.official@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or your questions by email.
